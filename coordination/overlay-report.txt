  Recommended/new main default 0.30; preserve existing saved settings.

  Native stiffness remains 200 / 200^effectiveSoftness; the 0.50 setting is about 5.6x

- Node mass = 0.65 + 2.35*t^2, t = authored vertical coordinate across the contour.
  Normalize total mass to 24; bottom nodes are heavier, shifting the actual mass centre
  below the geometric centre. Pin/tool lock and unlock restore these inverse masses.

  with a dead zone, bounded angular acceleration and weaker correction in free flight.
  No pose teleport; disabled while held, locked, sleeping or gravity-off.

  This fixes persistent drift in a pile against a side wall; floor-only friction was
